  1. Build PB07 Quad-vs-Hexa mass rollup using source-backed propulsion mass and 12S battery candidates

  3. Compare 12S3P vs 12S4P P50B and 14S alternatives for 10-minute mission energy and current
  4. Re-audit B1 3kW 48V 100V-MOSF DRV8353-class design against PB07 and identify KEEP RECALCULATE REPLACE blocks
