S1 3 BATTERY ARCHITECTURE

Sıradaki şema revizyonu: U1-SCH-R001 · Açılmış gerçek U1 revizyonu: 0

B1: frozen referenceU1-R000: boş iskeletU1-R001: henüz açılmadı

Sıradaki işler

  1. Shortlist exact 500A-continuous / 1050A-3s current-path components: main fuse, contactors, current sensor, service disconnect and output connector
  2. Build busbar/weld resistance and thermal budget using actual geometry candidates
  3. Define peak-capable SOC/temperature/SOH envelope and reconcile P50B/P60B/P45B candidates against 54V/500A and 1050A/3s requirements
  4. Obtain exact production motor Ld Lq or execute MOTOR IMPEDANCE MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE PB03 before PWM freeze
  5. Advance S1.4 environment and numeric OV/UV/OCP/OTP/watchdog/failsafe requirements where frozen parents permit

Neden bazı işler bekliyor?

  • Freeze PWM G1 07Exact production-motor Ld/Lq/effective ripple inductance remains unavailable; physical measurement or exact manufacturer winding data is required
  • Freeze exact battery packExact cell/topology, complete pack mass, cold/low-SOC/EOL sag, peak-capable SOC-temperature-SOH envelope and BMS/contactors/fuses require exact-pack design and/or physical evidence
  • Close G0 mass environmentAirframe battery fixed-equipment mass allocation and numeric environmental envelope remain open
  • Close exact switching loss and parallel countRequires final PWM plus guaranteed hot-resistance policy, switching waveform/energy correlation, transient ZthJC, current sharing and baseplate/TIM model
  • Allocate U1 SCH R001G1 and page-level G2 architecture remain open
